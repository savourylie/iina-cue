Cue · Local AI subtitles

Technical preview. Uses Gemma E2B and Qwen forced alignment; your video and captions stay on this Mac.

Manual activation and original-language captions are the defaults. Playback continues while captions are prepared, and Cue attaches them when ready. In the sidebar, you can change the subtitle and source language, adjust subtitle size, pause until captions are ready, or switch the translucent black background. Cue never resumes your own pause. The Plugin → Cue menu offers the same language choices.

First-time setup: run scripts/setup-dev, review the sources, terms, and roughly 3.56 GB of model weights shown by scripts/setup-models, then approve the download. This development build does not yet have a Terminal-free installer.

Permissions: read selected videos and write local captions, run a local helper, connect only to 127.0.0.1, and show status. No microphone, screen recording, cloud service, or telemetry.
